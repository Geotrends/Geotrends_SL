Welcome to the GeoTrends Analytics Platform

This platform lets you explore, analyze, and visualize strategic information from multiple sources, supporting decision-making in environmental, urban, social, and business contexts.

What do we use this platform for?

  • Centralize the management and analysis of data from sensors, platforms, and external sources.
  • Visualize spatial and temporal information dynamically through interactive dashboards.
  • Connect institutional, technical, and citizen actors around the strategic use of data.

User roles

  • Administrators: User and sensor management and general configuration.
  • Managers: Data analysis, report generation, and indicator tracking.
  • Citizens: Access to public sensor information and environmental reports.

How does GeoTrends contribute?

  • We design data-driven solutions, integrating technology, geography, and advanced analytics.
  • We offer flexible tools for exploring, downloading, and comparing key indicators.
  • We support decision-making processes with clear visualizations and specialized support.
